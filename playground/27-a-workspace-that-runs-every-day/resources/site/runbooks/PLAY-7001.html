PLAY-7001 — Failing over the payments database

Owner: platform on-call.

When

Fail over when the primary has not answered a health check for five minutes, or when replication lag on the standby is under ten seconds and the primary is degraded.

Steps

  1. Freeze deployments to the payment gateway.
  2. Promote the standby, and point the gateway's connection string at it.
  3. Confirm that new payments are written to the promoted database.
