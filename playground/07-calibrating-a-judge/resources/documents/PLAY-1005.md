# PLAY-1005 — Intermittent upload stalls, cause undetermined

**Severity:** major
**Resolved:** 2026-06-24
**Systems affected:** upload-service

## Timeline

- 2026-06-22 to 2026-06-24 — uploads over about 200 MB stall at a different
  percentage each time. Roughly one attempt in twelve is affected.
- 2026-06-23 — a proxy timeout, a client retry loop and a storage backend hiccup
  are each investigated. None of the three is ruled in or out.
- 2026-06-24 — the stalls stop after a routine node rotation. The incident is
  closed with the cause undetermined.

## Root cause

Undetermined. The competing hypotheses are an unbounded retry loop in the client
SDK and a proxy that closes idle connections mid-upload; the evidence does not
distinguish between them, and the stalls stopped before either could be tested.

## Impact

Support handled 31 complaints. The credits issued came to a recorded loss of
CHF 2'750.

## Follow-up

- Keep the upload traces for 30 days instead of 3, so the next occurrence can be
  distinguished.
