PLAY-4002 — Internal DNS resolver flapping under cache expiry

Severity: minor
Resolved: 2026-05-09
Systems affected: internal-dns, orders-api, search-indexer

Timeline

  • 2026-05-08 06:12 UTC — services begin logging intermittent NXDOMAIN for internal names that plainly exist.
  • 2026-05-08 06:55 UTC — the failures are found to arrive in bursts exactly five minutes apart.
  • 2026-05-08 08:30 UTC — one of the two resolver replicas is drained and the bursts stop.
  • 2026-05-09 — the replica is rebuilt from the current image and returned to service.

Root cause

One resolver replica had been running an image eight months old, which expired negative cache entries on a five-minute timer and refused to refresh them from the upstream zone. Half of all queries reached that replica, so half of the lookups for a recently created name failed until the entry aged out of the other replica too.

Impact

No orders were lost. The search index fell four hours behind and was rebuilt the same evening; no financial loss was recorded.

Follow-up

  • Fail the deployment when two replicas of one service report different image digests.
  • Alert on negative-cache hit rate rather than on query latency alone.
