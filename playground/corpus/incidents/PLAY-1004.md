# PLAY-1004 — Search latency spike during the spring campaign

**Severity:** major
**Resolved:** 2026-05-12
**Systems affected:** search-api, catalogue-index

## Timeline

- 2026-05-11 18:30 UTC — the spring campaign launches and search traffic triples
  within ten minutes.
- 2026-05-11 18:44 UTC — p99 search latency passes four seconds. Autoscaling adds
  pods, and latency does not recover.
- 2026-05-11 19:15 UTC — the catalogue index is found to be rebuilding on every
  pod start, so each new pod spent its first eight minutes serving from a partial
  index.
- 2026-05-12 — the index is moved to a shared read-only volume and the incident
  is closed.

## Root cause

Each pod rebuilt the catalogue index locally at start-up, so scaling out under
load added pods that served partial results for their first eight minutes and
made latency worse rather than better.

## Impact

Search was degraded for 45 minutes at the campaign's peak. The campaign team
recorded a loss of CHF 9'900 in attributed revenue.

## Notes

The traffic itself was within the capacity the campaign was planned for. The
autoscaler reacted as configured; nothing in this incident points to a
misconfigured autoscaler or to an attack.

## Follow-up

- Ship the index as a read-only volume built once per release.
- Load-test a scale-out event, not only steady-state traffic.
