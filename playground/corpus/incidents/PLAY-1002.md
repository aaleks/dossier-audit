# PLAY-1002 — Nightly export missed the reporting window

**Severity:** minor
**Resolved:** 2026-03-19
**Systems affected:** reporting-export

## Timeline

- 2026-03-18 02:10 UTC — the nightly export job starts 40 minutes late because
  the upstream warehouse load finished late.
- 2026-03-18 05:55 UTC — the export is still running when the reporting window
  closes at 05:30 UTC, so the downstream dashboard shows the previous day.
- 2026-03-19 — the job is re-run by hand and the dashboard is correct again.

## Root cause

The export job was scheduled at a fixed time rather than triggered by the
warehouse load it depends on, so any delay upstream pushed it past a window it
had no way of knowing about.

## Impact

No customer-facing impact. Two internal dashboards were a day stale for one
business morning. No financial loss was recorded for this incident.

## Follow-up

- Trigger the export from the warehouse load's completion event.
- Alert when the export has not finished 30 minutes before the window closes.
