PLAY-4005 – Connection pool exhausted by a report query

Severity: major
Resolved: 2026-06-21
Systems affected: orders-api

Root cause

A month-end report query held its transactions open for as long as the export ran, and the orders-api connection pool – forty connections – was exhausted within four minutes.

Impact

Orders placed through the Zürich storefront between 09:14 and 09:52 failed. The recorded loss is CHF 9'800.
