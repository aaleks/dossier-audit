PRODUCTION INCIDENT NOTE
Site: Distribution Centre 2
Severity: minor
Resolved: 2026-06-02
Systems: badge-reader

Badge readers at the north gate rejected valid badges for 40 minutes during the
early shift. The reader firmware had rolled back to a previous version after a
power interruption, and the badge format it expected was the older one.

No financial loss was recorded. Staff were let through manually by the shift
supervisor and the queue cleared within the hour.
